# Concept

Generate when source material describes mechanisms, loops, layers, flywheels, or abstract relationships. Use hand-authored SVG constrained by the lock and strong visual enhancement.

Choose `loop-mechanism` for a small feedback loop, `capability-map` for a conceptual hierarchy or matrix, `layered-system` for stacked mechanisms, and `control-sidecar-flow` only when an explicit control plane constrains a primary mechanism. Keep conceptual stages few and visible; move operational procedure into a companion flow diagram when it exceeds the loop budget.

A loop is proved by a locked backward secondary relation between named stages. An enclosing curve, orbit, or decorative ring does not count as feedback. Keep guardrails adjacent to their constrained stages and route stop conditions through the control rail.
