# Business Architecture

Generate when source material names business domains, capability layers, roles, or value streams. Prefer Mermaid or Graphviz and strong visual enhancement.

Choose `capability-map` for hierarchical or matrix structure, `value-stream` for ordered business stages, and `bounded-context-map` for domain ownership boundaries. Prefer alignment and containment over connector-heavy composition. Split capability structure from value-stream interaction when arrows begin to dominate the map.
